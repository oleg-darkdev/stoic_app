General Inquiries

What types of companies do you work with and in what industries?

We collaborate with a diverse range of businesses across Asia and worldwide markets, from ambitious startups ready to make their mark to established enterprises seeking a fresh perspective. Explore the full list of industries we have experience supporting.

How many people will be working on our account?

The size of your dedicated team depends on the project's scope and complexity. But rest assured, you'll always have a main point of contact to ensure seamless collaboration with our strategists, designers, and developers.

How do you manage client communication and collaboration?

We believe in transparent and open communication. We'll keep you in the loop every step of the way, with regular updates, progress reports, and opportunities for feedback. We use tools like Slack, Notion, and Figma to facilitate seamless collaboration and ensure everyone is on the same page.

Does Level™ offer ongoing maintenance after project completion?

Absolutely! We're committed to building long-term relationships with our clients. We offer various ongoing support and maintenance packages to ensure your brand and website remain effective and up-to-date.

Brand Identity

What is brand identity, and why is it important for my business?

Your brand identity is the soul of your business - the unique combination of values, personality, and visuals that sets you apart. It's how your audience perceives you, remembers you, and connects with you on an emotional level.

In today's competitive market, a strong brand identity is essential for building trust, fostering loyalty, and driving business growth. It helps you stand out from the crowd, attract your ideal customers, and command a premium for your products or services.

How does Level™ approach brand identity development?

We take a collaborative and strategic approach, diving deep into your business goals, target audience, and competitive landscape. We believe in unearthing your brand's authentic story and translating it into a captivating visual and verbal language that truly resonates. We'll help you define your brand fundamentals – its mission, story, and messaging – and craft a striking visual expression through logos, colors, typography, and imagery. Together, we'll build a brand identity that not only stands out but also fosters meaningful connections with your audience.

What does the brand identity design process at Level™ look like?

Our process is a journey of discovery and creation. It starts with research and strategy, followed by concept development, design iterations, and final delivery. We'll keep you involved at every stage, ensuring the final result perfectly captures your brand's essence.

  • Discovery: We get to know your business, your goals, your competition, and your audience.
  • Strategy: We develop a brand strategy that defines your positioning, messaging, and visual identity.
  • Concepts: We explore creative directions and concepts that bring your brand to life.
  • Design: We iterate on designs based on your feedback until we achieve perfection.
  • Delivery: We provide you with the necessary files and guidelines to implement.

How can a strong brand identity help my business stand out in competitive markets?

In today's fiercely competitive markets, a strong brand identity is your key differentiator. Whether in Taiwan, the greater Asia region, or elsewhere in the world, a strong brand identity helps you:

  • Cut Through the Noise: A unique and memorable brand identity grabs attention and makes you stand out from competitors.
  • Build Trust and Credibility: A consistent and well-defined brand fosters trust with your audience, establishing you as a reliable and reputable business.
  • Connect with Your Target Audience: A brand identity that resonates with your customers' values and aspirations creates an emotional connection that drives loyalty and advocacy.
  • Command Premium Pricing: A strong brand allows you to charge a premium for your products or services, reflecting the added value and perceived quality.

Can Level™ help with brand positioning and messaging?

Absolutely! We have extensive experience in helping businesses revitalize their brands. Whether you're looking to modernize your image, expand into new markets, or reposition your business, we'll guide you through a strategic rebranding process that ensures a smooth transition and maximizes impact.

Website Design

Why is web design so important for businesses today?

Your website is often the first impression customers have of your brand, acting as a digital storefront that represents your business to the world. A well-designed website not only enhances your brand image but also drives tangible results. It fosters positive user experiences, encouraging visitors to explore your offerings, stay longer on your site, and ultimately convert into loyal customers.

What makes a website effective?

An effective website is one that is user-friendly, visually appealing, and optimized for both desktop and mobile devices. It should be easy to navigate, with a clean and intuitive design that allows users to find the information they need quickly and efficiently. Additionally, it should clearly communicate your brand message, ensuring that visitors understand who you are and what you stand for. An effective website seamlessly blends aesthetics and functionality. It should be:

  • User-Friendly: Easy to navigate, with intuitive layouts and clear calls to action.
  • Visually Appealing: Aesthetically pleasing and aligned with your brand identity.
  • Optimized for Mobile: Responsive design ensures a seamless experience on all devices.
  • Fast-Loading: Quick page load times are crucial for user satisfaction and SEO.
  • Content-Rich: Provides valuable information that engages and informs your audience.

What platforms or technologies does Level™ use for web design?

We specialize in Webflow, a powerful no-code platform that lets us create custom, responsive websites without sacrificing design or functionality. As a certified Webflow partner and theme developer, we leverage its capabilities to bring your vision to life.

We also have extensive experience with other platforms, including Shopify and WordPress, ensuring we choose the best solution for your specific needs and budget.

Our goal is to help you rank higher in search results, driving more organic traffic and attracting potential customers. But it doesn't stop there. We'll also focus on enhancing user experience and engagement to keep visitors coming back for more.

Content Marketing

What is content marketing and how can it benefit my business?

Content marketing is the art of storytelling for your brand. It involves creating and sharing valuable, relevant content that educates, entertains, or inspires your target audience. By consistently delivering high-quality content, you build trust, foster relationships, and position your brand as a thought leader.

Benefits of content marketing:

  • Increased Brand Awareness: Reach a wider audience and stay top-of-mind with your target customers.
  • Improved SEO: Boost your website's search engine rankings with optimized content, driving more organic traffic and potential customers.
  • Lead Generation: Attract, engage, and nurture potential customers through the sales funnel with valuable content.
  • Customer Loyalty: Foster strong relationships and encourage repeat business by providing ongoing value to your audience.
  • Thought Leadership: Establish your brand as an authority and trusted resource in your industry.

How does Level™ approach content marketing?

We believe in a data-driven and results-oriented approach. We kick off with thorough research to understand your audience, industry, and competitive landscape. From there, we craft a customized content strategy that outlines your target keywords, ideal content formats, distribution channels, and measurable KPIs.

What types of content marketing services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of content marketing services, including blog writing, social media management, video production, email marketing, and more. We can also help you with content distribution and analytics to ensure your content reaches the right people and achieves your desired outcomes.

We offer a full suite of content marketing services tailored to your brand and goals:

  • Content Strategy Development: We'll create a roadmap tailored to your brand and goals, outlining target keywords, content formats, distribution channels, and measurable KPIs.
  • Content Creation: Our team of writers and designers will craft engaging content (blog posts, articles, social media content, videos, infographics) that resonates with your audience and drives action.
  • SEO Copywriting and Optimization: We'll ensure your content is both captivating and search engine-friendly, helping you attract organic traffic and reach a wider audience.
  • Content Distribution and Promotion: We'll get your content in front of the right eyes through strategic social media promotion, email marketing, and other channels.
  • Content Marketing Analytics and Reporting: We'll track key metrics and provide regular insights to measure the success of your content and guide future strategies.

What is the difference between content marketing and traditional advertising?

Traditional advertising interrupts the user experience with direct product or service promotion. Content marketing, on the other hand, focuses on providing value and building relationships through informative, educational, or entertaining content. It's a subtler, more sustainable approach to attracting and engaging your audience.

How can content marketing help generate leads and increase sales?

Compelling content attracts and engages potential customers, nurturing them through the sales funnel. By offering valuable insights and addressing their pain points, you establish trust and credibility, making them more likely to choose your products or services. Content marketing also helps boost your online visibility and brand awareness, leading to increased traffic, leads, and ultimately, sales.

How does Level™ ensure that print designs are consistent with my brand identity?

We believe in creating a cohesive brand experience across all touchpoints. We'll start by deeply understanding your brand's visual language, including your logo, color palette, typography, and overall aesthetic. From there, we'll craft print designs that seamlessly integrate with your existing brand identity, ensuring consistency and recognition.

How can print design complement my digital marketing efforts?

Print and digital marketing are a powerful duo. Print materials can drive traffic to your website, social media, or online store, while digital channels can generate leads for printed brochures or catalogs. We'll help you develop an integrated marketing strategy that leverages both mediums for maximum impact.

Is print design still relevant in today's digital age?

Absolutely! Print offers a tangible, tactile experience that digital can't replicate. It's perfect for building brand awareness, showcasing products, and making a statement at events. From business cards to billboards, print remains a vital tool for connecting with your audience in the real world.
