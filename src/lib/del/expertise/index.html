Process

Step 1

Research & Discovery

We start by understanding your needs and thoroughly analyzing the market. Through extensive research, we gain insights into your target audience, industry trends, and competition. This helps us create a strategy to meet your needs and position you for success in the market.
  • In-Depth Market Research
  • Identify Problems & Challenges
  • Positioning & Differentiation Analysis
Step 2

Strategy & Planning

This step is all about formulating a unique angle for your brand. After understanding your background and market situation, we develop a strategy to help you achieve your brand objectives. We work closely with you to align on this strategy and create a project plan that defines roles, milestones, and deliverables. This plan will guide us moving forward.
  • Strategy & Positioning
  • Project Scope of Work
  • Milestones & Project Plan
Step 3

Execution & Delivery

This step is focused on taking action. We will begin delivering the items outlined in the scope of work, following the project plan. To keep you and your team informed, we will have weekly project syncs. Additionally, we will maintain constant communication through Slack and Notion, which are two cloud-based tools we use to effectively guide projects forward.
  • Ideate & Present Design Concepts
  • Design Creative Assets & Source Files
  • Deliver All Assets & Source Files
